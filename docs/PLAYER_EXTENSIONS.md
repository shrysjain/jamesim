# Player controls, sessions and robot extensions

Implemented on `feature/player-controls-robots-match-rules`, preserving the fixed-step simulation and existing view/UI boundaries.

- Settings now persist keyboard mappings, controller button mappings, enablement and stick deadzone. Left stick translates; right-stick X turns. Keyboard aliases are shown in Help. Enter/Escape/H remain reserved. Buttons default to browser standard mapping: LB intake, RT shoot, RB reverse, A raise, B lower, X supply, Back restart, Start pause. Input resumes only after controller neutrality following pause, focus loss or disabled periods.
- Session exposes timed match/free practice, robot, alliance, left/center/right spawn, 0–8 preloads, event profile and optional outpost stock. Startup still immediately starts the existing blue 2910 match. Restart repeats the selected session and seed. Session choices are held for the current visit; controls/settings persist across visits.
- Robot definitions now carry capacity, intake rate, intake compression behavior, shooter lanes/rate, turret, moving-shot and climb capabilities. Both robots reuse the same chassis, fuel, shooter and match simulation. Owned fuel retains its stable body/ID through robot switches and restarts.
- 4414 High Tide is selectable with an original procedural placeholder, single-lane turret and moving-shot support. **Its capacity (24), intake rate (20/s), fire rate (8/s), mass and geometry are estimates, not verified FRSim values.** The site supplied by the user, https://rebuilt.frsim.com/, is interactive; its text snapshot exposes no 4414 card, and direct runtime retrieval returned HTTP 403. The official team binder, https://2026.team4414.com/, confirms a turret, rotor feeder and moving-shot architecture. Do not present this placeholder as team CAD or an accurate FRSim port. No unverified climbing capability is assigned to 4414.
- An original Tower trainer exercises climb support separately. Approach your own tower at X approximately ±6.7, Z ∓0.289, settle, then hold Raise. Release to hold; Lower descends and detaches at the bottom. A physical fixed joint to a kinematic tower anchor supports the chassis. Mechanisms do not shoot while attached. Tower levels use bumper-bottom clearance over low/middle rungs; rates, attachment corridor and geometry are gameplay approximations. No robot may attach to the opposite tower.
- Tower assessment occurs after AUTO/final processing. AUTO awards 15 points; teleop levels award 10/20/30. Fuel targets exclude tower and penalty points. Traversal is 50 tower points and cannot be reached by one robot (maximum 45). Solo results omit competitive win/tie RP. Early termination includes tower assessment.
- Optional human-player stock transfers 12 existing neutral fuel per alliance into outpost ownership after preloading. Supply releases at 2/s through a clearance-tested outlet. Stock is finite, disabled periods pause supply, blocked outlets retain ownership, and restart replenishes from the same configured supply. This is a simplified manual outpost feed, not a human-player AI or full expanded championship field layout.
- The manual foul dialog awards minor/major 5/15 points to the opponent. Intent-dependent human conduct cannot be inferred reliably from solo physics. Opponent collision/protection rules, cards/DQ and tournament adjudication are not simulated. Legal aim assistance prevents outside-zone hub shots rather than manufacturing a penalty event.

Rule references: FIRST 2026 manual §§6.5.2–6.5.3 and Team 254 Cheesy Arena `game/score.go`, `game/rule.go` on its main branch, retrieved November 1, 2026. Existing driver-operated AUTO, assessment gaps and processed-fuel windows remain intact.
