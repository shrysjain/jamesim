# Player controls, sessions and robot extensions

Implemented on `feature/player-controls-robots-match-rules`, preserving the fixed-step simulation and existing view/UI boundaries.

- Settings now persist keyboard mappings, controller button mappings, enablement and stick deadzone. Left stick translates; right-stick X turns. Keyboard aliases are shown in Help. Enter/Escape/H remain reserved. Buttons default to browser standard mapping: LB intake, RT shoot, RB reverse, A raise, B lower, X supply, Back restart, Start pause. Controller button rebinding swaps conflicting assignments. Input resumes only after controller neutrality following connection, pause, focus loss or disabled periods.
- Session exposes timed match/free practice, robot, alliance, left/center/right spawn, 0–8 preloads, event profile and optional outpost stock. Startup still immediately starts the existing blue 2910 match. Restart repeats the selected session and seed. Session choices are held for the current visit; controls/settings persist across visits.
- Robot definitions now carry capacity, intake rate, intake compression behavior, shooter lanes/rate, turret, moving-shot and climb capabilities. Both robots reuse the same chassis, fuel, shooter and match simulation. Owned fuel retains its stable body/ID through robot switches and restarts.
- 4414 High Tide / Ripcurrent is selectable with a procedural hopper/rotor/turret model, single-lane aiming and moving-shot support. The [official technical binder](https://2026.team4414.com/) confirms 85+ fuel below the trench, a 25×32 inch frame and a 3 inch flywheel. The simulation uses 85 separated 150 mm fuel slots, dedicated pickup geometry and shooter pivot/radius. Bumper allowance, module placement, mass, intake/fire/turret rates and exact geometry remain estimates. The supplied [FRSim site](https://rebuilt.frsim.com/) is interactive and its text snapshot does not expose the 4414 card; exact FRSim parity and team CAD are still unverified. No climbing capability is assigned to 4414.
- An original Tower trainer exercises climb support separately. Approach your own tower at X approximately ±6.7, Z ∓0.289, settle, then hold Raise. Release to hold; Lower descends and detaches at the bottom. Attachment requires facing the tower and a physical raycast hit on its geometry. A fixed joint uses the detected attachment point on a kinematic support. Mechanisms do not shoot while attached. Tower levels use bumper-bottom clearance over the upper surface of the 1.66 inch OD low/middle rungs and reject other supporting field contacts; rates, attachment corridor and geometry are gameplay approximations. No robot may attach without tower geometry, while facing away, or to the opposite tower. Support travel is capped at 1.14 m to stay within the tower upright height.
- AUTO tower assessment occurs at the 20 second boundary; final assessment occurs when the solo robot rests or the three-second grace expires, whichever comes first. An early stop during AUTO assesses AUTO tower points. Completed results reject subsequent tower mutations. AUTO awards 15 points; teleop levels award 10/20/30. Fuel targets exclude tower and penalty points. Traversal is 50 tower points and cannot be reached by one robot (maximum 45). Solo results omit competitive win/tie RP. Early termination includes tower assessment.
- Optional human-player stock transfers 12 existing neutral fuel per alliance into outpost ownership after preloading. Supply releases at 2/s through a clearance-tested outlet. Stock is finite, disabled periods pause supply, blocked outlets retain ownership, and restart replenishes from the same configured supply. Loose fuel returned through the outpost is recovered for reuse. The HUD shows alliance stock and blockage. Supply clearance runs after the world step so newly added obstacles are included in physics queries. This is a simplified manual outpost feed, not a human-player AI or full expanded championship field layout.
- The manual foul dialog awards minor/major 5/15 points to the opponent. Intent-dependent human conduct cannot be inferred reliably from solo physics. Opponent collision/protection rules, cards/DQ and tournament adjudication are not simulated. Legal aim assistance prevents outside-zone hub shots rather than manufacturing a penalty event.

Rule references: FIRST 2026 manual §§6.5.2–6.5.3 and Team 254 Cheesy Arena `game/score.go`, `game/rule.go` on its main branch, retrieved November 1, 2026. Existing driver-operated AUTO, assessment gaps and processed-fuel windows remain intact.
