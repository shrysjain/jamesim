## Implemented behavior

The registry adds Prismarine to the existing singleplayer selector. It has four articulated swerve modules, a translating intake/rack carriage, animated intake/shooter rollers, rotating indexer, turret aiming, one physical shooting lane and alliance-colored CAD bumpers. It starts stowed and passively extends. V toggles retraction when cargo permits; Shift/Q extend for collection/reverse. Shooting keeps the intake out and waits for translation to settle. There is no enabled climber or adjustable hood.

Cargo uses full-size non-overlapping fuel seeds and the shared sphere hopper solver. The feeder follows floor-to-center-to-turret waypoints before releasing the same owned Rapier body. Capacity is an estimated **40 fuel**; nominal intake and shooting targets are **24 fuel/s** and **8 shots/s**, not independently measured team performance. Simultaneous collection/shooting is supported by the shared mechanism model.

## CAD preparation and placement corrections

Keep the original CAD locally at `assets/source/robots/8592-prismarine/production-main.glb` (ignored), or pass its path explicitly:

```sh
node src/robot/robots/8592-prismarine/prepare.mjs "ProductionMain (1).glb"
```

Use Node 26 and pnpm 10.20.0 with the frozen lockfile. The script verifies the input SHA-256, transforms source `(X,Y,Z)` to runtime `(Y-.005,Z-.223,X-.00081)`, builds semantic assembly nodes and writes the GLB, runtime record and ignored preparation report. It is deterministic with the pinned dependencies; player runtime only loads the prepared model.

Corrections and cleanup:

- Remove **both** embedded `FRC Game Piece` occurrences, including the purple fuel; remove camera FOV solids, the detached shooter-ball-path guide and CAD origin cat.
- Bring the three retained detached feeder shaft/pulley solids down by 520.7 mm to the feeder deck; omit their small disconnected bearings/spacers with other internal hardware.
- Seat the two detached camera mounts opposite the existing pair around the chassis center. These placements are reconstructed from the seated mounts, not verified production camera extrinsics.
- Place the detached signal light and its short support bar on the upper frame. Their final mounting positions are visual estimates.
- Normalize the turret's approximately 3.085° source offset and heading to neutral +X; the November 2 reference-photo correction flips the earlier backwards visual heading by 180°. Its measured bearing height and physical shot direction stay consistent.
- Normalize all four exported module steering angles together, preserving original tread/fork geometry and giving every wheel axle the required +Z direction.
- Separate actual carriage, indexer and roller geometry at their measured centers. Fixed intake actuation mounts stay on the chassis.

Tiny fasteners, bearing detail and spacers are omitted. Per-part meshes are welded on a 10 µm grid, simplified with a 3 mm positional error budget, batched by mechanism/material palette and Meshopt-compressed. Source surface normals and numbered bumpers are retained. The November 2 reference photos set cyan pixel vinyl over dark carbon, dark teal frame members, orange printed parts, black rollers/belts, silver shooter side plates and white CAD bumper lettering that stays white when the alliance changes. Two small deterministic PNG textures are generated by `appearance.mjs`, embedded into the GLB and projected onto the broad CAD panel faces. They approximate the wrap colors/pattern; sponsor lettering is not reproduced. The preparation report lists removed/corrected source indices and assembly bounds.

Prepared payload: **1,496,988 bytes**, **325,659 rendered triangles**, **140 primitives**. The exact hash and bytes are generated in `runtime.json`; all resources are embedded. Original CAD, downloaded code excerpts and preparation reports remain outside the production pack.
